## 🧠 Technical Summary
Agent Herbie is fundamentally designed to solve the challenge of deploying and operating AI agents within secure, physically isolated networks (air-gapped). This necessitates a complete reliance on local infrastructure for computation, data processing, and model inference, bypassing external cloud services entirely. The technical approach focuses on packaging the necessary runtime, models, and operational framework into a self-contained unit that can be deployed locally, acting as a closed-loop operational infrastructure layer for AI agents where external connectivity is prohibited or impossible.
