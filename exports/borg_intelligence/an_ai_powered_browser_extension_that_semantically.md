## 🧠 Technical Summary
ClusterTabs utilizes AI-driven tagging to automate the organization of browser environments by processing tab metadata, specifically page titles and URLs. The tool identifies semantic relationships between open web resources and clusters them into discrete windows, effectively isolating distinct workstreams or research topics. This approach minimizes cognitive load and session entropy by maintaining logical context boundaries across high volumes of concurrent information.
