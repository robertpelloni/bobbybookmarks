## 🧠 Technical Summary
A self-hosted, lightweight "memory cache" PWA designed for the rapid capture, categorization, and retrieval of digital thoughts, links, and files.

## 🛠️ Key Features
Message-style rapid capture inbox, multi-clipboard organization, PWA cross-platform sync (Docker deployed), Apple Shortcuts / Android Share native integration.
