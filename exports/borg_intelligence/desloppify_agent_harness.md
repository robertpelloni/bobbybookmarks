## 🧠 Technical Summary
An open-source harness that combines mechanical linting with subjective LLM review to provide agents with a strict "beautiful code" score to chase.

## 🛠️ Key Features
Mechanical/Subjective combined review, "North Star" quality score, stateful `desloppify next` loop, 29+ language support.
