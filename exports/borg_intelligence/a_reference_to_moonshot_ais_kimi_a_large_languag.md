## 🧠 Technical Summary
The Kimi model architecture, developed by Moonshot AI, focuses on the engineering challenges of 'long-context' processing. It utilizes specialized attention mechanisms and memory-efficient KV cache management to support windows ranging from 200,000 to over 2 million tokens. This technical approach allows for the ingestion of entire codebases, long-form legal documents, or complex technical manuals within a single inference session, minimizing the need for external RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) by maintaining high retrieval accuracy (Needle In A Haystack) across massive data sequences.

## 🛠️ Key Features
Million-token context window, efficient KV cache optimization, high-fidelity long-range dependency retrieval, multi-document reasoning, specialized tokenization for large sequences, lossy context compression.
