## Methodology

1. **Source**: 10,665 borg-researched entries in the Borg Intelligence Database
2. **Threshold**: Innovation score ≥ 7 (6,805 entries qualify)
3. **Classification**: LLM-assigned `category` field maps entries to 7 canonical Borg categories
4. **Cross-cutting**: Text matching identifies entries for 7 additional domains (harness, security, vector, etc.)
5. **Subcategories**: Keyword-based classification within each layer
6. **Standout**: Entries with innovation ≥ 9 AND quality ≥ 0.7 are marked 🏆/⭐
7. **Cross-references**: Each entry shows how many other layers it appears in
8. **Security list**: Strict — requires security in description/title, not just tags (eliminates noise)
